How MYB LIMITED handles information associated with this website.

Last updated: 1 October 2026

MYB LIMITED is a private limited company registered in England and Wales (company number 17466047). This policy explains what information may be handled when you use this website or contact us.

Information you provide

If you contact us, you may provide your name, email address, company name, and the contents of your message. The website contact form prepares this information in your own email application; the website itself does not submit or store the form data.

Why information is used

Information sent to us by email may be used to respond to your enquiry, understand your requirements, provide information about relevant services, maintain appropriate business records, and protect legitimate business interests.

Legal basis

Depending on the context, personal information may be handled because you have asked us to take steps before entering into a contract, because it is necessary for a contract, because we have a legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries and operating our business, or because we must meet a legal obligation.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. Information may be handled by service providers used for business communications or website hosting where necessary. We retain enquiry information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was received and for relevant legal or record-keeping requirements.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies. The hosting provider may process limited technical data, such as IP addresses and request logs, for security, delivery, and reliability purposes under its own policies.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you may have rights in relation to your personal information, including rights of access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection, and data portability where applicable. These rights can depend on the circumstances and may be subject to legal exceptions.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email abedalrah99@gmail.com. Please do not send sensitive identity, financial, or tax documents through the website.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy when our website or practices change. The updated date above indicates the latest version.
